Billing questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

Yes — Pro trials start through secure Stripe checkout. You are not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then from the billing portal.

What happens when I run out of credits?

New runs are paused until the next month or until you upgrade. Running jobs always finish.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep your plan until the end of the billing period, then move to Lite. Your projects stay.

Do you offer academic or volume pricing?

Yes — email us about student team, classroom and enterprise licences, including on-premise deployment.
